# Privacy

Prey Chrov runs entirely in your browser.

- **No accounts, no tracking.** The game has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, cookies or sign-in.
- **No network requests while you play.** Code, fonts, art and sound all come with the game, or are generated on your device. The only requests are for the game's own files, from the site that hosts it. The host may keep ordinary web-server logs (for example, your IP address and the time of the request), as for any website.
- **Saves stay on your device.** Journeys, settings and achievements are stored in your browser's local storage for this site. They are never uploaded.

## What is stored

| Key | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `preychrov.save`, `preychrov.save.2`, `preychrov.save.3` | Your three journey slots: world seed, position, inventory, buildings, progress and the journey's rules |
| `preychrov.slot` | Which slot you last played |
| `preychrov.settings` | Volumes, accessibility options and key bindings |
| `preychrov.records` | Achievements, completion counters and endings reached |

## Deleting your data

- To delete one journey, open the main menu → **Journeys…** → **Delete**.
- To delete everything, clear this site's data in your browser. In Chrome or Edge, click the icon left of the address bar, then **Site settings → Delete data**. In Firefox, use **Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data**.

Private or incognito windows usually delete local storage when they close. If your browser blocks storage completely, you can still play, but journeys can't be saved. The menu tells you when this happens.

Local storage belongs to one browser on one device. Saves don't move between devices or browsers.
