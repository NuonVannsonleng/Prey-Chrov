# Prey Chrov: Player Guide

*ព្រៃជ្រៅ — The Deep Forest.* You wake in a deep forest inspired by Cambodian jungle and folklore. Stay alive, find the five stone tablets, and renew an old promise at the Heart of the Forest.

The game is a work of fiction. Its spirits and places are creative interpretations, not descriptions of real Khmer religion.

## Starting

1. Choose **Play** on the menu. If you already have a saved journey, choose **New journey** instead.
2. Choose your rules for this journey. They can't be changed later.
   - **World seed:** leave it random, or type a word or number to get the same world again.
   - **Difficulty:**
     - **Story** is gentle and focused on the tale.
     - **Survival** is the intended game.
     - **Hard** brings more, sharper-eyed creatures and faster hunger.
   - **Challenge modifiers** are optional twists. For example, **Lean Season** means less food and **Cold Monsoon** means harsher rain.
   - **Permadeath** is off unless you switch it on. With it on, falling ends the journey and deletes its save.
3. Choose **Begin**. The help panel on the left lists the controls. Press **H** to hide it.

## Controls (defaults)

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move (Shift: sprint) |
| E (hold) or left click | Gather, drink, cook, sleep, open, read |
| Space, or click near a creature | Attack |
| F | Eat or use the held item, or place a held building |
| 1–6 / mouse wheel | Choose a hotbar slot |
| I · C · J · K · M | Inventory · Crafting · Journal · Character · Map |
| Z (hold) | Crouch: quieter, and hidden in bushes |
| R · X | Rotate or repair · Dismantle (press X twice) |
| Q | Drop one item |
| Esc | Close panels, or pause |

You can rebind every key in **Settings → Controls**, and the in-game hints update to match. The menus also work with the arrow keys and Enter.

## Your first day

1. **Gather:** hold E on trees, bamboo, rocks and bushes. Berries and bamboo shoots are food.
2. **Craft** (C) a stone axe, then a campfire. Place the campfire with F.
3. **Drink** at any water (E). Cook raw food at the campfire, because raw meat can make you ill.
4. Before night, build a **shelter** and stay near your fire. Spirits (Ap and Arak) will not enter firelight. A torch keeps them back while you walk.
5. **Sleep** in the shelter (E) to pass the night and save your journey.

## Staying alive

- **The bars:** health, food, water and stamina. In the monsoon, **warmth** matters too, so stay under a roof or by a fire.
- **Creatures warn you first:** every attack has a visible wind-up. Step aside, then strike.
- **Stealth:** crouch (hold Z) to stay quiet, and hide in bushes. A **?** means a creature heard something. A **!** means it saw you.
- **Neak Ta shrines** are guardian places, never enemies. Leave a little food as an offering for a short blessing.

## The journey

- **Stone tablets:** ancient stones tell the story. Your journal (J) keeps what you have read.
- **The map** (M) shows where you have been. Click to add markers. Landmarks, ruins and shortcuts appear on it as you find them.
- **Ruins** hold puzzles and hidden caches. Watch for spike traps, which flash before they rise.
- **Side stories** begin from things you do. Their choices change the world and the ending.
- **Great trials:** the Old Tusker, the Great Storm and the Blight at the Heart. Each is telegraphed and has a trick: lure the boar into a tree, take shelter from the lightning, and burn the blight's cores with fire.
- **The Heart of the Forest:** when you have read the tablets, carry fire to open the root ring. Make the offering, and you can keep playing afterwards.

## Saving

- The game **autosaves** when you sleep, pause, or reach a safe moment. Autosave is skipped while you are in danger.
- **Journeys…** on the menu holds three save slots. **Continue** resumes the slot you last played.
- Saves stay in this browser only. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Settings and accessibility

- **Audio:** master, effects, ambience and music volumes.
- **Display & access:** reduced motion, reduced flashes, high contrast, large text, and help on start.
- **Controls:** rebind keys. A key already in use swaps with the other action.

## Records

**Records** on the menu lists 24 achievements, most of them for discovery, and shows how much you have completed. They are kept in this browser only.

## If something goes wrong

- If a message says "Something went wrong", choose **Reload**. Your saved journeys are kept.
- If the menu says browser storage is unavailable, you are probably in a private window or storage is blocked. You can still play, but you can't save.
